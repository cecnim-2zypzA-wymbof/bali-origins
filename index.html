Rooted in the island

Good souvenirs shouldn’t come with a hidden story.

Bali Origins is built around a simple belief: visitors deserve the real thing, at a fair price. We source coffee from Kintamani’s highlands and botanical teas from Balinese growers—without inflated tour commissions or confusing claims.

Each 100g tin is sealed with an inner aluminum foil pouch and one-way degassing valve—designed for freshness, gifting, and easy packing in your luggage.
